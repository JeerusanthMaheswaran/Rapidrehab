How it works

Four phases. You move on when the numbers say so.

Each phase ends with a measurable test: range of motion, strength symmetry or a hop test. That's how we keep recovery fast without rushing it.

  1. 01 Visit 1

    Assess

    History, movement screen and baseline measurements. You leave with a diagnosis and a written plan.

  2. 02 Weeks 1–2

    Calm

    Settle pain and swelling with manual therapy and gentle range work you can do at home.

  3. 03 Weeks 3–6

    Load

    Progressive strength training until the injured side tests within 10% of the other.

  4. 04 Week 6 +

    Return

    Sport-specific drills and return-to-play testing, then a plan to keep you there.

Questions
Before you book.

      

        
Do I need a doctor's referral?
No. You can book directly. If your insurer needs a referral for a claim, we'll tell you at the first visit.

        
How many sessions will I need?
It depends on the injury. After your assessment you'll get an expected timeline and a number of sessions, which we update as you hit each phase test.

        
What should I wear?
Something you can move in. Shorts for knee, hip and ankle problems; a vest or sports top for shoulder and neck problems.

        
Can you work with my surgeon?
Yes. Bring your post-op protocol and we'll follow it, then send progress updates back to your surgical team.

        
What if I need to cancel?
Give us 24 hours' notice and there's no charge.
