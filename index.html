FAQ

        
Common questions

        
Anything else? Call us at 647-901-8676.

      

      

        
Do I need a doctor's referral?
You don't need a referral to see a physiotherapist in Ontario. Some insurance plans ask for one before they'll pay, so check your plan or give us a call.

        
Which insurance do you accept?
We accept extended health insurance, motor vehicle insurance and workplace safety insurance. Bring your policy details to your first visit.

        
I was in a car accident. Can you help?
Yes. We treat motor vehicle accident injuries such as whiplash, back pain and soft-tissue injuries. Bring your claim information when you come in.

        
What should I bring to my first visit?
Photo ID, your insurance details, any doctor's notes or imaging reports, and clothing you can move in comfortably.

        
Can I get orthotics or compression stockings without a treatment plan?
Yes. Call to book a fitting. Many extended health plans cover custom orthotics and compression stockings.
